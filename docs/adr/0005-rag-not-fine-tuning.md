- **Status:** Proposed — accept at the Week 2 design review

- **Deciders:** BACKEND, FRONTEND, AI
- **Affects:** Weeks 15-16, the explanation engine, the out-of-scope section of the thesis

## Context

TODO — write this in Week 1 together, in your own words. It is the part the
committee reads. Seed points:

- Fine-tuning needs (incident -> correct remediation) pairs. A few hundred public post-mortems exist in total; that corpus cannot be constructed.
- Fine-tuning would cost roughly 150 hours plus GPU budget out of ~1,450 net build hours.
- Explanations must cite sources. Retrieval gives citation for free; a fine-tuned model gives fluent unsourced text.

## Decision

Explanations are generated by retrieval-augmented generation over a curated
corpus plus the tenant's own history, with the model constrained to cite only
provided sources. The model is not fine-tuned. `ExplanationEngine` also has a
template implementation so recovery never depends on an LLM being up.
